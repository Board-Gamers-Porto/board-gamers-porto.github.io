# Event photo audit — 14 September 2026

Reviewed all 79 image references across the eight weekly galleries from 27 July
through 14 September and the 29 August monthly gallery.

None of the downloaded images contains EXIF capture timestamps. The 45 images
with XMP/IPTC metadata contain HDR gain-map information and Facebook processing
data, not capture dates. Instagram post dates establish when images were posted,
not when they were taken. Event attribution below uses dated recap captions,
poster dates, visual comparisons and the user's September 14 correction.

Promotional posters remain with the event they advertise, even when their design
includes an older photograph. Standalone photographs in announcement carousels
must be checked separately.
